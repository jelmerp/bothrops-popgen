## Why

`confident` never looks at how intermediate the depth ratio is. It's only two checks, at [04b_sex-and-z-class.sh:380-383](scripts/04b_sex-and-z-class.sh#L380-L383):

```awk
ok    = (auto[s] >= mindp)                                        # mindp = 15
agree = (sex == "female" ? (hd < 0.0005 || gs > 0) : (hd >= 0.0005))
```

- `ok` can never fire — the lowest autosomal depth in the run is 29.6.
- The **female branch is effectively vacuous**: `gs > 0` (any heterozygous gametolog site) is true for every female in the data (range 132-190). So no female can be flagged, at any ratio.
- The **male branch** only asks whether hemizygous het density is high enough.

So the one flag, I12279 (ratio 0.8803), is a male whose hemizygous het density is 0.000333 — below the 0.0005 cutoff, i.e. female-like het signal on a solidly male ratio. That's a real oddity, just not the kind you were expecting to see flagged.

The three intermediates pass for the opposite reasons:

| sample | ratio | sex | het_hemi | n_het_gametolog | why it passed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1507 | 0.579 | female | 0.00226 | 144 | `gs > 0` escape hatch |
| I1795 | 0.585 | female | 0.00126 | 179 | `gs > 0` escape hatch |
| I1495 | 0.613 | female | 0.00378 | 139 | `gs > 0` escape hatch |
| I1624 | 0.745 | male | 0.00337 | 24 | het density ≥ 0.0005 |

The three females all have *male-like* hemizygous het density (males run 0.0007-0.005), which the `||` waves through. I1624 is called male but has 24 heterozygous gametolog sites — more than any other male (0-16) — and a ratio 0.055 below the male cluster.

The gap-split itself is fine: largest gap is 0.6133→0.7445 (0.131). But the main female cluster actually ends at 0.5254, with a 0.053 gap before those three — they're their own island.

## Suggested fix

Two changes to the confidence rule:

1. **Add a ratio-margin check** — flag samples whose ratio sits more than some distance from its own group's core (e.g. outside median ± k·MAD of the assigned group, or within X of the split point).
2. **Fix the female branch** — `hd < 0.0005 || gs > 0` should not be an OR. Since every sample has some gametolog hets, the discriminating signal is hemizygous het density alone (females ~0-0.002, males ~0.001-0.005) plus gametolog het count relative to the group.

## Sex-call confidence — [scripts/04b_sex-and-z-class.sh](scripts/04b_sex-and-z-class.sh)

Replaced the old rule with within-group robust z-scores. Since sex is assigned by a gap split, the only thing that can be judged is how well a sample sits inside the group it landed in. Three statistics are z-scored within the assigned group using the median and a MAD-based SD (`--max_group_z`, default 3); a statistic whose group MAD is zero is skipped (the usual case for female het density):

- `depth_ratio` — catches samples between the two modes
- `het_density_hemi` — heterozygosity over the differentiated Z
- `n_het_gametolog` — the female-specific signal
