## The threshold

After the Z is removed the het-density distribution is smooth and unimodal, with no gap anywhere:

| | value | × median |
|---|---|---|

A percentile removes a fixed 1% by construction, whether or not anything is anomalous. And you already have a calibration point for what a real collapsed paralog looks like: the 4 Z gametologs sit at **0.029–0.049**, i.e. 8–14× the autosomal median. The p99 cutoff is at 2.7× — well below any real paralog signature.

## Per-site het is the statistic that actually separates paralogs

Per-locus density conflates "diverse" with "paralogous". The paralog signature is *near-fixed heterozygosity at diagnostic sites*, so I computed, for every site in all 1762 loci, the fraction `f` of called samples that are het (≥50 calls required). Under HWE the max is 0.5, so `f > 0.6` is already excess.

- 1682 loci have **zero** such sites; 65 have 1–2; only 15 have ≥3. Median frac = 0, p95 = 0, p99 = 0.002. That is signal vs. no signal.
- Of your 18 flagged loci, **14 have zero sites with `f > 0.6`** (max `f` 0.38–0.65) — indistinguishable from random control loci. L690, the very top of the density distribution, is one of them.
- Only **L1813, L473, L1306, L663** carry the signature.
- Loci at density ranks 85, 113, 184, 566, 880, 1077 (L1826, L1914, L476, L1727, L1720, L1652) *do* carry it and are currently retained.

## BLAST cross-check — yes, and no re-run needed

[blast_out_raw.tsv](results/blast_probes/blast_out_raw.tsv) is unfiltered (down to 70.6% id, 2% qcov). Scoring each probe by best off-target hit bitscore ÷ best hit bitscore (`sec_ratio`, off-target = different chrom or >10 kb away):

- 3.5% of all loci exceed 0.4, but **22% of the 18 flagged loci do** — 6-fold enrichment.
