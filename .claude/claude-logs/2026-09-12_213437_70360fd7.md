**No, not entirely.** The 300-bp buffer is right, and so is the claim that the buffer is called (98% of buffer positions have a genotype). The rest has problems:

1. **The intervals are trimmed.** The depth figures in this section come from 04a, which runs on `retained_loci.bed` ([run/1_main.md:265](run/1_main.md#L265)). 03c shifts those coordinates inward using the ClipKIT trim log ([03c_filter-loci.sh:319-325](scripts/03c_filter-loci.sh#L319-L325)), so the intervals are not probe plus the full 600 bp. In the final loci:
   - **Short loci:** median length 553 bp (not 800), with a median of 353 bp of buffer left.
   - **Long loci:** a median of 310 bp of buffer left.
   - **Overall:** about 34% of the bases counted are buffer.
   - The trimming mostly removes the outer, lowest-depth part of the buffer.

2. **"~70x on the probe" only holds for long probes.** 04g gives 44.5x on short probes and 69.8x on long probes. Short loci are 1,265 of the 1,697 final loci. The buffer is 24.8x on short loci and 27.5x on long ones. Those buffer figures are for the full, untrimmed 300 bp, so the buffer that is actually left is somewhat deeper.

3. **"Well under" overstates the gap.** Weighting the 04g depths by the retained probe and buffer bases gives about 51x over the retained intervals, against about 64x on the probes alone. The real gap is smaller still, because the remaining buffer is deeper than 25x. The median per-sample `mean_dp` is 47x. "Somewhat below" is closer.

4. **"Average over whole locus intervals" is loose.** `mean_dp` is the mean FORMAT/DP over called genotypes, not over every position.

5. **"Comparisons between samples are unaffected" is only nearly true.** "Every depth figure in this section" also covers the per-locus plots, and between loci the buffer share matters a lot; your *Locus lengths* section says so. Between samples the effect is small but not zero. Across the 125 samples in `results/vcf/filt`, buffer depth divided by probe depth ranges from 0.36 to 0.48 (median 0.40). It is only weakly related to insert size (r = −0.16).

Suggested replacement:

> The depth figures in this section are mean FORMAT/DP over called genotypes within the retained locus intervals. These are a capture probe plus a 300-bp buffer on each side, after ClipKIT trimmed the poorly covered ends. A median of ~350 bp of buffer remains on short loci and ~310 bp on long ones. The buffer is much less deeply covered than the probe (~25x over its full width, against ~45x on short and ~70x on long probes; see *Locus lengths* below). These numbers are therefore lower than on-probe depth, and loci of different lengths are not directly comparable. Between samples the effect is small: buffer depth is 0.36–0.48 of probe depth in every sample.
