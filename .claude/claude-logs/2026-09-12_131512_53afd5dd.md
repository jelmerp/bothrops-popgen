## What the plot shows

x = reads per sample (millions), y = `mean_dp` (mean `FORMAT/DP` over called sites, from 04a), color = % duplicates. The slope of each line is **depth yielded per million reads** — the efficiency with which sequencing effort converts into on-target coverage.

## Why two lines

The two clusters are **two capture/sequencing batches**, identified by flowcell in the FASTQ headers:

| | flowcell | n | slope (dp per M reads) | mean % on-target | mean reads | mean % dup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| upper line | run 605, `HCTGLBCXX` | 68 | 17.0 | 18.8 (range 13.3–25.4) | 2.81 M | 6.2 |
| lower line | run 705, `H2HLLBCXY` | 57 | 8.6 | 10.2 (range 8.7–11.9) | 3.63 M | 3.3 |

The split is entirely `pct_on_target`. Across all 125 retained samples, depth-per-read correlates with `pct_on_target` at r = 0.998, and at 0.988 *within* each batch. The two batches don't overlap at all on that metric (13.3–25.4% vs 8.7–11.9%), so batch 605's capture was ~1.8× more efficient and its samples get ~2× the depth per read.

The batches also separate on the x-axis: 705 was sequenced ~30% deeper, partly compensating for the worse capture. That's why the lower cloud sits down and to the right rather than just below.

Two other things worth knowing:

- **The color is confounded with batch.** Duplication is higher in 605 (4.3–14.4%) than 705 (2.4–7.5%), so the viridis gradient largely re-encodes batch rather than showing an independent effect. Duplication is *not* what causes the split — within batch 605, the correlation between duplication and depth-per-read is only −0.10 (within 705 it's −0.56, a real but secondary effect).
- **One sample crosses over.** `I1619` is on flowcell 605 but falls in the lower cloud: it has the dataset's highest duplication (14.4%) and the lowest on-target rate of its batch (13.3%).

## Looks like batch, is actually geography

This is the important one. Per-sample **heterozygosity** — the readout [05b_qc-loci.qmd:842-912](scripts/05b_qc-loci.qmd#L842-L912) worries about, and what 05c is built on — differs by batch within *jararaca*: 0.492% vs 0.445%, p = 1.1e-4. The samples were batched by collection region. So this is real geographic structure, not an artifact, but the confounding also means the two can't be cleanly separated. Of 05c's groupings, `state` is the one to treat with care (species p = 0.007, land type p = 0.14, sex p = 0.25).
